materials & process

From shoreline to shelf

Every shell is gathered by hand along the coastline near Vladivostok, then cleaned and sorted before it ever reaches the studio table. Nothing is bleached or smoothed — the natural ridges and markings stay.

  • 01
    Gather & cleanCollected shell by shell, washed, and sorted by shape and thickness.
  • 02
    DecoupageA chosen paper motif is applied by hand and sealed under lacquer, layer by layer.
  • 03
    Gild the edgeThe natural edge is finished in gold leaf, following the shell's own line — never straightened.
  • 04
    Set & finishPearls, stoppers, or incense fittings are set by hand, then the piece is sealed and cured.
about

A continent ends here

Vladivostok sits at the edge of a continent, where a vast country finally meets open water. The Pacific here is cold and grey-blue, often wrapped in fog. Ships move through the bay. Hills drop straight into the water. Winters are long.

We work with shells gathered from this specific coastline — cleaned, decoupaged, and edged in gold by hand, the same way a jeweler would set a stone. No two are alike, because no two stretches of coastline are either.

The gold marks the edge. Everything inside it, the sea already made.

Each piece is meant to travel — a small, permanent fragment of the coast, set into another room, another city, another life.
