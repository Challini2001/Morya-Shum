materials & process

From a restaurant to shelf

Many of the shells are collected from restaurants — what was meant to become waste just yesterday becomes part of an interior today, taking on a new life in a different form. This approach is very close to my heart: creating something new without generating unnecessary waste, while working with what already exists in nature and the urban environment.

  • 01
    Gather & cleanCollected shell by shell, washed, and sorted by shape and thickness.
  • 02
    DecoupageA chosen paper motif is applied by hand and sealed under lacquer, layer by layer.
  • 03
    Gild the edgeThe natural edge is finished in gold leaf, following the shell's own line — never straightened.
  • 04
    Set & finishPearls, stoppers, or incense fittings are set by hand, then the piece is sealed and cured.
about

Made by the sea, shaped by a creative soul

Bringing a little sea into your everyday. This project was born from a simple wish: to work with your hands, to choose the creative over the productive. Shells felt like the right material – slow sunlit days, salt on the lips, the particular stillness that only the sea can give.

A little gold, a small detail, a new shape – and a shell once tossed aside becomes a stand for jewelry, a stopper for wine, a decoration for the tree, or a candle poured in pearl-soft wax. Nothing wasted, everything given a second life.
